OWNER CHECKLIST

7 operational issues that quietly destroy exit value

Not a pitch deck. A straight checklist for service-business owners who want the business to run — and sell — without depending on them every day.

  1. 01

    The business only works when you’re in the room

    If decisions, relationships, and firefighting still sit with you, buyers discount hard. Ask: what breaks in two weeks if you step back?

  2. 02

    Critical know-how lives in people’s heads

    Undocumented processes = risk. If a key manager left tomorrow, could someone else deliver the same result without calling you?

  3. 03

    Customer experience depends on who is on shift

    Inconsistent CX across sites or teams shows up in reviews, retention, and diligence. Buyers pay for repeatable quality, not heroics.

  4. 04

    No clear second line of leadership

    Without managers who can own outcomes, the business isn’t transferable. Succession isn’t a speech — it’s capability on the floor.

  5. 05

    Sales and pipeline live in spreadsheets (or nowhere)

    Unpredictable revenue and messy CRM data weaken confidence. Clean operating rhythm beats “we’re always busy.”

  6. 06

    Numbers and ops don’t tell the same story

    If financials, job data, and delivery reality don’t reconcile, diligence slows — and value leaks. Hygiene is an exit asset.

  7. 07

    No written path from “today” to “without me”

    Hope isn’t a transition plan. Owners who win exits know the sequence: audit gaps → 90-day priorities → build → hand over.
