THE REALITY

Most service businesses are not sellable — or only sell at a discount.

Buyers and successors pay for predictable cash flow, transferable systems, and leadership that doesn't depend on the founder.

Without these, even profitable businesses struggle to achieve strong valuations or smooth transitions.

Common valuation killers
  • • Owner is the only one who knows how things really work
  • • Inconsistent service and guest experience across sites
  • • High staff turnover with no documented processes
  • • Revenue that walks out the door when key people leave
THE EXIT-READY FRAMEWORK

Six pillars that turn operations into a valuable asset

1. Transferable Systems

Complete, living SOP library and knowledge management so the business doesn't rely on tribal knowledge or any single person.

2. Leadership Depth

Successor identification, development plans, and delegation frameworks so the business runs at the same standard when you're not there.

3. Consistent CX at Scale

Standardised customer experience that delivers the same quality across every site, shift, and team member — the foundation of brand value.

4. Owner-Independence Score

Clear measurement of how much the business depends on you personally, with a roadmap to dramatically reduce it.

5. Financial & Operational Hygiene

Clean reporting, documented processes, and controls that give buyers and successors confidence in the numbers and operations.

6. Transition Playbook

A structured plan for the actual handover — whether that's a full sale, family succession, or phased step-back over several years.
